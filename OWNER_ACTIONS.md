# Owner actions: Activity growth and Cloud validation

Everything below requires the founder's identity, judgment, accounts or direct
contact with users. Product code, documentation, test automation and launch copy
can be delegated; these actions cannot be completed credibly by automation alone.

## Do now

- [ ] Confirm that the public product name remains **Activity** and that
      `feedclip.dev` remains exclusively about FeedClip.
- [ ] Decide whether to keep GitHub Pages as the public Activity home for the next
      three months or buy a separate Activity domain. Do not use a FeedClip path.
- [ ] Record the [60-second launch video](docs/launch-kit.md#60-second-launch-video)
      in your own voice and review it for secrets or customer data.
- [ ] Select 20 B2B SaaS/internal-tool prospects with invoice, order, customer or
      ticket detail pages.
- [ ] Send the first five personalized messages using the outreach draft; record
      replies and follow-ups outside the public repository.

## During the first five integrations

- [ ] Attend or conduct each session; do not test only your own repositories.
- [ ] Record time to first event, time to durable storage, changed host files,
      blockers and production intent using [the study kit](docs/adoption-study.md).
- [ ] Ask permission separately before using a person's name, company, logo,
      quotation or measured result.
- [ ] Offer free integration help first; offer paid production setup only after the
      user confirms value.

## Launch accounts and publishing

- [ ] Publish the prepared Show HN post yourself and answer comments personally.
- [ ] Choose one React/Next.js community whose rules allow showcases and adapt the
      Reddit/community draft rather than cross-posting identical text.
- [ ] Publish the technical article and social announcement from your accounts.
- [ ] Enable GitHub Discussions if you want a public showcase/support channel.
- [ ] Add a support or consulting contact method that you are prepared to monitor.

## Activity Cloud decisions

- [ ] Choose a separate Activity Cloud public name and future hostname; it may use
      shared infrastructure but must not appear as a FeedClip feature.
- [ ] Approve which existing identity, billing and project services may become
      product-neutral internal platform modules.
- [ ] Choose three design partners and agree on retention, volume and integration
      requirements before building a polished dashboard.
- [ ] Validate the €29/100k-events and €99/1m-events hypotheses through interviews;
      do not publish them as commitments yet.
- [ ] Review tax, invoicing, privacy, DPA, deletion and retention obligations before
      accepting production customer data.
- [ ] Approve Stripe products/prices only after measured infrastructure cost and at
      least one explicit willingness-to-pay signal.

## Decision gates

- [ ] Do not build Activity Cloud beyond the vertical slice until three design
      partners have a real use case.
- [ ] Do not add enterprise features until one buyer requests and values them.
- [ ] Do not publish a customer story without written approval.
- [ ] Continue investing when users reach a real first event without author help and
      at least one team is willing to pay for managed operations.
